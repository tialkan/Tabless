<h1 align="center">Less friction. More hospitality.</h1>

<p align="center">A simpler digital experience for independent cafés and restaurants.</p>

Tabless is being rebuilt around a calm, mobile-first experience for guests and business owners.

- Browse and order without an account or app.
- Turn an existing menu into a reviewed digital draft with AI assistance.
- Print table QR cards at home.
- Follow orders on a kitchen phone or tablet.
- Choose optional guest accounts for useful extras, never as an ordering requirement.

**Status:** the new design and interactive product preview are in development. Live business accounts, AI import and paid plans are not open yet. Features above describe the intended product, not a claim that the current live site implements them.

**Planned offer:** 30 days free, no card needed. Continue on a paid plan only if you choose to. No commission on orders and no backdated trial bill.
